Direct Sync Options:
Requires the Zotero desktop app open (Zotero 10+), with Settings → Advanced → “Allow other applications on this computer to communicate with Zotero” turned on. Clicking Connect will pop up a permission dialog inside Zotero — choose “Always Allow” so you don't have to confirm again next time.
Create it at zotero.org/settings/keys → “Create new private key” → tick “Allow write access”.

Link & export

waiting
Your manuscript is never uploaded to any server. The tool only (a) reads the .docx locally in your browser, (b) queries public metadata APIs (Crossref / OpenAlex / Semantic Scholar / DataCite) for reference lookups, and (c) creates standard Zotero CSL field codes in your exported document.
Works seamlessly with Zotero 6, 7 & 10+ via .bib import or direct sync. Open the exported .docx in Word and click Refresh in the Zotero ribbon tab to activate your references.
