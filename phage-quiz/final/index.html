⏱ Every question auto-starts a 45 s timer: 15 s reading (options blurred & locked) then 30 s answering (options unlock with a chime). Ring turns green in the answer phase, pulses red and ticks in the last 5 s, and a buzzer + TIME UP stamp fires at zero. Rapid-fire questions get a straight 30 s.
